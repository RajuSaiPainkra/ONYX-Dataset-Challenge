# ONYX Dataset Challenge

## About ONYX Data

ONYX Data is a UK-based company dedicated to delivering cutting-edge data analytics and business intelligence solutions. The company is well-known for fostering data-driven innovation and empowering individuals to excel in the field of data science through its flagship initiative, the DataDNA Dataset Challenge.

## What is the ONYX Dataset Challenge?

The ONYX Dataset Challenge is a monthly competition designed to engage data enthusiasts, professionals, and students in real-world data analysis and visualization tasks. It provides participants with a platform to:
<br>
<ul>
    <li>Showcase their skills in data analytics and information design.</li>
    <li>Build connections within the data science community.</li>
    <li>Solve practical problems using real datasets.</li>
</ul>


## Key Features of the Challenge
<ul>
    <li>Skill Enhancement: <br>Participants analyze datasets to uncover insights and create compelling visualizations.</li>
    <li>Career Development: <br>A great way to gain recognition and boost your professional portfolio.</li>
    <li>Community Building: Engage with a global network of data enthusiasts</li>
</ul>

## Why Participate?
<ul>
    <li>Learn and Grow:<br> Enhance your data analytics and visualization skills.</li>
    <li>Win Prizes:<br> Compete for exciting rewards, including eBooks, vouchers, and software licenses.</li>
    <li>Gain Recognition:<br> Get your work noticed by industry professionals and peers.</li>
</ul>


## How to Get Involved?

To participate in the ONYX Dataset Challenge, follow these simple steps:
<ol>
    <li>Download the dataset provided for the current month's challenge.</li>
    <li>Analyze and visualize the data using tools like Power BI, Tableau, Python, or R.</li>
    <li>Submit your entry by sharing your work on LinkedIn and completing the official submission form.</li>
</ol>
